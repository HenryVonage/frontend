Part 2

Build your own DJ mix

Pick a vibe and a length, and the demo plans a real DJ set — not a shuffled playlist — then delivers it to your WhatsApp or RCS conversation.

  • Harmonically mixed: every transition is key-compatible (Camelot wheel) and the tempo follows a club arc — warm-up, build, peak, landing.
  • Five vibes, three lengths: chilled, house party, madness, workout or motivational — 30, 60 or 90 minutes.
  • Two sources: Henry's own record crate (no sign-in), or your own Spotify library (approved testers — the app is in Spotify's preview mode).
  • What you get: a Spotify playlist in set order, the set sheet (start time · track · BPM · key) and an "arc" chart of the set, all in the chat. Turn on Spotify's Crossfade and it plays as one continuous mix.
  • Spotify only: tempo and key come from Spotify's own track data; nothing else is downloaded or scraped.
💬 Prefer to stay in the chat? Just type "mix" — e.g. "a 30 min chilled mix" or "workout mix for an hour" — in the WhatsApp or RCS conversation you started in part 1.
